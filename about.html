📸 Lens&LayeredDesigns Shoots

We don’t just snap — we capture motion, mood, and machines in their zone. If it rides, we shoot it.

🔧 Shoot Flow Breakdown

  • Pre-Shoot Planning: Lock in location, lighting, and must-have edits.
  • Gear Setup: Tripod for stills, gimbal for silky motion — always vibed to match the energy.
  • The Shoot: Wide shots, badge close-ups, rim angles, and those cinematic rollers.
  • Editing: Polished in Lightroom, Photoshop, and Premiere — InPixio for extra sauce.

🎒 Our Gear Loadout

  • Canon T7: Sharp, color-true images with high-res detail.
  • Tamron Lens: Adaptable focal range — perfect for macro or full-frame.
  • Tripods & Gimbal: Locked or in motion, always smooth.
  • Editing Stack: Adobe Suite + InPixio for creative finesse.
